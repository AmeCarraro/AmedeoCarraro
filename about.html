About

Everything that does not fit on the first two pages: who I am, what I studied, what I know, and the years I spent behind a bar.

In short

Technology has been my thing for as long as I can remember. I started with the fundamentals, as a software developer, and moved step by step towards machine learning: first neural networks for images and time series, then large language models, which are what I build with today.

What I like most is the stretch between research and production: taking a technique that works in a notebook and making it work for the people who have to use it, within the limits of real hardware, real data and real deadlines.

I am Italian, I live near Vicenza and I work at Terya, in Bassano del Grappa. Outside work I go to the gym, play team sports, cook and read.
