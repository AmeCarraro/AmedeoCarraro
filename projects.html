Deep learning and LLMs

Variable star classifier

Source ↗

MSc thesis · University of Padua · 2024 – 2025

Variable stars change brightness over time, and the shape of that change tells astronomers what kind of star it is. I trained two PyTorch models on light curves from the Zwicky Transient Facility (ZTF): a 2D-CNN that reads each curve as an image-like representation, and a hybrid CNN-LSTM that reads the reconstructed sequence. Both were evaluated with 5-fold cross-validation on 5-class and 10-class setups.

2D-CNN: 99.95% accuracy on the full 5-class dataset, 88.7% on 10 classes. CNN-LSTM: 90.0%, with training about three times faster.

  • PyTorch
  • 2D-CNN
  • CNN-LSTM
  • Time series
  • ZTF

Recipe generation with Mistral-7B and QLoRA

Source ↗

Group project · NLP course, University of Cyprus · 2024

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of Mistral-7B-v0.1: the model is loaded in 4-bit and adapted with LoRA through Hugging Face PEFT, so it trains on a single GPU. Given a list of ingredients, it writes a recipe title and the instructions. We ran five training rounds, from 200 to almost 20,000 recipes, improving data cleaning and prompt format each time.

Evaluated with BLEU, ROUGE-1 and METEOR: 62%, 81% and 78%.

  • Mistral-7B
  • QLoRA
  • PEFT
  • Hugging Face

PCB defect detection with YOLOv8

Source ↗

Personal project · 2025

A YOLO model trained on the DeepPCB dataset to find six kinds of defects on printed circuit boards: open, short, mousebite, spur, pin hole and spurious copper. One script runs the whole pipeline, from data preparation and train/validation/test split to training and testing. A FastAPI service exposes the trained model, and Docker packages it.

  • FastAPI

  • DeepPCB

Leukemia cell classifier

Source ↗

Two-person project · AI in Medicine course, University of Cyprus · 2024

Classifies white blood cells as healthy or leukemic (acute lymphoblastic leukemia) on the C-NMC 2019 dataset. Instead of a convolutional network, it uses OpenCV preprocessing and augmentation, handcrafted features and a small Keras neural network tuned with grid search, which needs far less compute.

F1 score 84.66%, against 77.43% for an SVM on the same features.

  • OpenCV
  • TensorFlow / Keras
  • Feature engineering
